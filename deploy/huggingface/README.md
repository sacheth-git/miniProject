# HireX

Semantic resume–job matching with skill-weighted sentence embeddings, plus a campus placement (TPO) mode.

- Upload a resume and get real job postings ranked by how well your skills fit, with the reasons and the skills you're missing.
- Placement officers run campus drives: eligibility, ranked blind shortlists, rounds, offers and analytics.

**Demo campus.** "Westfield Institute of Technology (Demo)" is fictional: 120 synthetic students and six drives.
Sign in as its placement officer with `tpo@demo.skillmatch.example` (password in the project's `backend/scripts/seed_demo.py`).

**Note.** This Space runs on free hardware: it sleeps when unused (about a minute to wake), and accounts created here
are wiped whenever the Space restarts. Job listings are a snapshot from the official APIs listed in the project report.

Source code: https://github.com/sacheth-git/miniProject
